# Identification-rule audit

Replays the adduct, charge, and isotope arithmetic in `data/feature_rows/*_neutral_mass_hypotheses.csv` against the rules written down in [docs/identification_rules.md](../../../identification_rules.md). Generated by `tools/audit_identification_rules.py`; read-only, and reproducible from committed inputs.

10,568 hypothesis rows over 4,483 features and 2,482 controls.

10,534 of 10,568 rows (99.7%) carry the neutral mass of a singly charged, monomeric, monoisotopic protonation, whatever ion their label names.

## Charge state and multimers

These two are separated out because their error is a multiplicative factor rather than a tolerance miss: a doubly charged precursor read as singly charged is out by roughly a whole molecular mass, a dimer by half of one. No mass window catches either.

| Family | Rows | Controls | Stored mass follows the label | Median error vs label |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `multiply_charged` | 269 | 194 | 0 | 458.27 Da |
| `multimer` | 450 | 235 | 0 | 144.83 Da |

The `charge` column agrees with the label on 5,988 rows and `multiplier` on 5,988, so the metadata is right and only the mass derived from it is not.

`recomputed_mz` equals `parent_mz` exactly on 8,774 rows, is blank on 1,794, and disagrees on 0. It echoes its input rather than round-tripping the neutral mass back through the adduct, so it cannot fail.
